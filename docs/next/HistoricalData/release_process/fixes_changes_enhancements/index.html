Fixes, Changes, and Enhancements

Occasionally SpiderRock improves data that has already been published — to correct a value, add a new field, or extend history. This page explains how each kind of update reaches v8_latest and v8, so you know what to expect and when.

Both locations hold stable data. v8_latest is simply the leading edge: it receives improvements to history first. v8 receives the same improvements after a 30-day adoption window, which gives you time to review an update and decide when to take it.

Types of Update​

Every update is recorded in the DataUpdateLog. Large or impactful updates to your subscribed datasets are also announced by email.

How Updates Reach You​

How an update is released depends on whether it changes only history, or also changes how new trading days are produced. The DataUpdateLog column impFwdData tells you which: No for history only, Yes if data going forward is also affected.

History-Only Updates​

Most updates correct or extend history without changing how new data is produced.

  1. Released to v8_latest. The updated files are published to v8_latest and the update is recorded in the DataUpdateLog with its v8LatestEffDate.
  2. 30-day adoption window. The update is available in v8_latest only. Use this window to review the update, test it against your own processes, and decide when to adopt it — either by reading the affected dates from v8_latest now, or by waiting for the promotion to v8.
  3. Promoted to v8. After 30 days, the update is promoted to v8 in the next weekly promotion run, replacing the prior version of each affected file. The v8EffDate in the DataUpdateLog is the promotion date.

Only files whose contents actually change are re-published during a promotion, so re-syncing v8 doesn't re-download unchanged files.

Example

DataUpdateLog entry 1125 removed expired options that remained in OptionIntradayHist on 213 trading dates between August 7, 2020 and October 7, 2021.

DateEvent
November 4, 2026Corrected history released to v8_latest.
November 4 – December 4, 2026Adoption window. v8_latest has the corrected history; v8 is unchanged.
December 5, 2026Promoted to v8. Both locations now identical.

New trading days are not affected (impFwdData = No).

Updates That Also Change Data Going Forward​

Some updates change both history and how data is produced from now on — for example, schema changes that add columns to a dataset or remove them. These updates reach you in two parts on different schedules. In the DataUpdateLog they have impFwdData = Yes.

Partv8_latestv8
New trading daysUpdated from the go-live date.Updated from the same go-live date.
History (dates already published)Updated from the go-live date.Updated after the 30-day adoption window.

Because new trading days in v8 change on the go-live date, SpiderRock emails clients in advance describing the change and its go-live date, so you can prepare your processes before it takes effect.

Example: adding new columns​

In July 2026, five columns were added to OptionPrintSetHist: a numeric print type that maps to OPRA print codes (prtTypeInt), and high-order Greeks at the time of print (prtVa, prtVo, prtPh, prtDeDecay).

How the change reached each location:

Whenv8_latestv8
Before July 18, 2026Advance notice emailed to clients.Advance notice emailed to clients.
July 18, 2026 (go-live)New trading days include the new columns, and history is populated with them.New trading days include the new columns. Files for earlier dates do not have them yet.
July 18 – August 21, 2026 (adoption window)New columns available across full history.New columns available from July 18 onward only.
August 22, 2026 (promotion)No change.History promoted. New columns available across full history — both locations identical.
If you use v8

Until the promotion date, v8 files before the go-live date won't include the change. For a schema change, that means older files may be missing an added column or still contain a removed one. Make sure your reader handles both, or read history from v8_latest during the adoption window.

What This Means for Your Pipeline​

  • On v8: Watch the DataUpdateLog and your email for advance notices. You have at least 30 days to review any historical update before it reaches v8. When a promotion runs, re-sync the affected dates.
  • On v8_latest: Updates arrive as soon as they are released. Re-sync the dates listed in each DataUpdateLog entry as soon as it appears.
  • Testing a change: Point a test job at v8_latest during the adoption window to validate a new field or correction before it reaches v8 history.

Full History Datasets​

Some datasets are published as a single Full History file that is rebuilt in full every day, rather than one file per trading date. These files are published to v8 only — they are not available in v8_latest.

DatasetFull History filePublished
StockCloseMarkHistStockCloseMarkHist_AllHist04:00 CT T+1
TickerHistory3TickerHistory3_AllHist05:00 CT T+1
ReturnFactorsHistReturnFactorsHist_AllHist05:00 CT T+1

Full History files are exempt from the 30-day adoption window. Because the whole file is regenerated each day from the current data, an update appears in the next day's Full History file in v8. Updates to these datasets are still recorded in the DataUpdateLog.

Fixes at Delivery​

The process on this page applies to improvements to data that has already been delivered. If a file is found to be incomplete or incorrect shortly after delivery, it is handled as a delivery issue instead: the fix is pushed directly to both v8 and v8_latest, is not recorded in the DataUpdateLog, and is communicated through a delay notice. See Data Delays.
