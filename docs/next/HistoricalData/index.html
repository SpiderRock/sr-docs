Historical Data

SpiderRock offers a comprehensive suite of historical data products across equities, futures, and options, built for clients who need reliable market data for modeling, analysis, and backtesting.

This section is organized around the questions most clients ask, in the order they usually ask them:

Key Features​

  • Robust Market Data & Analytics – Extensive historical market data and analytics that support data-driven decisions.
  • Point-in-Time (PIT) Accuracy – Data is captured and preserved as point-in-time records, avoiding look-ahead bias.
  • Comprehensive Documentation – Every dataset has a data dictionary entry with its schema, coverage, and delivery schedule.
  • Quality Assurance – Daily data quality checks verify completeness, validity, and accuracy.
  • Data Quality Indicators – Error codes and surface quality parameters flag cases where input data does not support an accurate options analytic calculation.
  • Transparent Change History – Every fix, backfill, and schema change is recorded in the DataUpdateLog.

Stocks​

Our historical stock datasets recap each day's trading activity in the underlying security. Files show open, high, low, close, and volume data for stocks, ETFs, and indexes. Delisted stocks are included for backtesting purposes.

  • Stock Close Marks – Taken either 1 or 5 minutes before market close, and updated when exchanges publish official marks. Includes closing quotes and prices, prior-day data with corporate action adjustments, and security IDs that link symbol data to corporate reference information.
  • Stock Minute Bars – Created once per minute for each open stock and index market, with high/low, spread, print, and bid/ask data.

  • Stock Imbalances – Buy and sell imbalance records during auctions for NYSE and NYSE Arca listed securities.

SpiderRock continually computes implied volatility surfaces for every option expiration with live market quotes. Surfaces are available end-of-day and at 5-minute and 30-minute intraday intervals.

  • Surface ATM – Surface points resampled onto a standard grid centered on the at-the-money point.
  • Surface Grid (Skew Surfaces) – Volatilities sampled across fixed expirations and delta strikes, with expected earnings volatility removed.
  • Surface Fixed Term – ATM volatility term structure with earnings effects removed, plus implied earnings moves (iEMove) and skew slopes so you can reconstruct earnings-affected volatility for any expiration.

European Data​

SpiderRock offers historical data for European stocks, equity options, futures, and options on futures, with the same schemas, analytics, and delivery methods as our US data. European data is published under the EU region (s3://srhistdatastore/<stage>/EU/, Snowflake schemas V8_EU and V8_EU_LATEST), partitioned by exchange, with date_p in Central European Time. All EU datasets are delivered once daily at 04:00 CET T+1 (MarketHoursConfigHist at 06:00 CET T+0).

Analytics Background​

Historical Greeks​

Our historical datasets include the full set of option Greeks:

  • Delta – Rate of change of option price for a 1-point change in the underlying price.
  • Gamma – Rate of change of Delta for a 1-point change in the underlying price.
  • Vega – Rate of change of option price for a 1% change in volatility.
  • Theta – Rate of change of option price for a 1-day change in time to expiration.
  • Rho – Rate of change of option price for a 1% change in the interest rate.
  • Phi – Rate of change of option price for a 1% change in the dividend rate.
  • Vanna – Rate of change of Delta with respect to changes in volatility.
  • Volga – Rate of change of Vega with respect to changes in volatility.

Implied Volatility Calculations​

SpiderRock uses a family of proprietary pricing models to compute prices, implied volatilities, Greeks, and scenario risk slides for equity and futures options. These models are solutions (sometimes numeric) to the generalized Black-Scholes equation, combined as a patchwork of sub-models that address the different regions of the skew curve and different option types.

Volatility Surfaces​

Theoretical volatility surfaces model the fair value of an option between the bid and offer. SpiderRock recomputes surfaces for every expiration as new quotes arrive. Each curve record contains the spline parameters for the surface shape, the calibrated at-the-money volatility, the dividend rate, any price offset, and related curve adjustments. Every SpiderRock options dataset includes theoretical surface prices and volatilities for each strike.
