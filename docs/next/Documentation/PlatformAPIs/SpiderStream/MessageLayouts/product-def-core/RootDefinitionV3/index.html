Layout: RootDefinitionV3 (ID: 9112)

One row per option root: its underlier, its delivery and exercise terms, and its pricing conventions. Sourced from the listing exchange for future options and from OCC for US equity options, and updated as SpiderRock receives changes. Field 100 ticker is the MASTER UNDERLYING - the containing complex - which is not the same key as the root itself.
