Layout: ProductDefinitionV2 (ID: 4360)

SpiderRock normalized exchange futures contract definitions. An option series is ProductExchMappingV3 (9130) under its RootDefinitionV3 (9112) and OptExpiryDefinitionV3 (9121); a spread is SpreadDefinitionV3 (9300) and SpreadExchIDV3 (9303). TickerDefinitions, RootDefinitions and CCodeDefinitions are consistent with these records.
